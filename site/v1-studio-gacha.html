Fair by design

Nothing hidden in the capsule.

Packs live or die on trust, so every number is public.

95%

Back in tokens

Of every pack price, 95% buys the five tokens. 5% is the fee: half buys back and burns the token every hour, on a public ledger.

Seed

Drawn before you pay

We publish the hash of our seed first. Your purchase signature finishes the draw, so neither side can pick the pull. Re-run any draw yourself.

Clean

No bundles in Common

Fresh launches enter a pack only after a full anti-rug trace: who bought the first ten seconds, who funded them, the dev's past launches.
