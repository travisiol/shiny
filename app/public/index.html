Published before you pay

Every ball has its own table. The multiplier is what your five tokens are worth when it pops.

Every ball has the same five prizes: Common x0.5, Rare x1 (your SOL back), Epic x1.8, Legendary x5 and Jackpot x10. Bigger balls trade small wins for big ones: more Legendary and Jackpot, more Commons. A Common still returns half the price in real tokens: there are no empty balls.
